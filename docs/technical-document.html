Executive Summary

▼
O3L — One Ocean One Life is a cross-platform digital ocean and coastal community platform connecting fishermen, coastal communities, researchers, NGOs, educational institutions, vendors, tourism stakeholders, and government/emergency resources across Tamil Nadu's coastline.

Core philosophy: "One Ocean. One Life." The platform enables: DETECT → LEARN → CONTRIBUTE → CONNECT → EARN → PROTECT → RECOGNIZE

▼
