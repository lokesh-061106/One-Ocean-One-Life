Why O3L

Built for the Coastal Ecosystem

A comprehensive suite of tools designed specifically for the needs of Tamil Nadu's ocean and coastal communities.

🌊

Interactive Ocean Mapping

GIS-powered MapLibre maps with real-time coastal data, fishing zones, weather overlays, and marine sanctuary boundaries across 14 districts.

🐟

Marine Species Guide

Comprehensive database of 500+ Tamil Nadu marine species with high-resolution images, habitats, migration patterns, and conservation status.

🏪

Seafood Marketplace

Direct-to-consumer marketplace connecting local fishermen with buyers. Cart, vendor storefronts, and live Razorpay/Stripe checkout.

🤝

Community Network

Connect with fishermen, researchers, NGOs, vendors, and government bodies. Events, forums, leaderboards, and public contributor profiles.

⛰

Ecotourism Portal

Discover beaches, book tour packages, and support sustainable coastal tourism. Provider dashboard with Razorpay pay-ins and split payouts.

🚨

Emergency & SOS System

Real-time SOS alerts, coast guard contacts, and emergency coordination. Location-based alerts for coastal safety and disaster response.

🎵

Ocean Sound Library

Authentic ocean soundscapes recorded from Tamil Nadu coasts. Surf ambience, whale calls, and coastal soundscapes for relaxation and education.

📚

Education & Research Hub

Marine biology courses, research papers, educational resources, and certification programs for coastal community empowerment.

📊

Analytics & Research

Annual and monthly trend reports, year comparison, variance analysis, and research-grade data export for agencies and scientists.

📝

Unified Contributions

One wizard for sightings, sounds, catches, observations, events, and cleanups — reviewed by moderators and scientists, then published across the platform.

🌱

SHG & Circular Economy

Self-help group microsite with livelihood strategies, revenue trackers, loan schemes, recipes, fresh-fish flows, and circular-economy ideas.

🧴

Beach Cleanups

Public cleanup campaigns with signup, photo galleries, and impact tracking — open on web and mobile for volunteers and NGOs.
